## Hi there 👋 I'm Gewei Cao

I'm an economist and doctoral researcher at the [Max Planck Institute for the Study of Crime, Security and Law](https://csl.mpg.de/en) in Freiburg, Germany. I work at the intersection of **law and economics**, **applied microeconomics** and **machine learning**, with a focus on labor economics, from historical slavery to modern human trafficking and political/institutional economy in China.

### 🔭 What I'm working on
- **Law and economics of labor disputes in China.** How do Chinese courts enforce labor law? 
- **Slavery as interpersonal asymmetric dependency.** Why did some societies integrate the enslaved while others kept them in permanent bondage? A cultural-evolutionary model tested with ethnographic data. 🗺️ [Interactive map](https://yudingshechu.github.io/indigenous-slavery-map/)
- **Modern slavery and mistrust.** Does human trafficking erode trust? Evidence from Romania and India. 📄 [Working paper](https://doi.org/10.26481/umagsb.2025008) (2nd prize, Elsevier/IAREP Best Student Paper Competition 2025)

### 🧭 How I work
I start from a real-world concern rather than a gap in the literature. I find evidence on hidden topics, formalize the mechanisms behind them, and test them with data. Each project is a long-term effort with several outputs: research papers, interactive multimedia presentations, and accessible essays for a wider audience.
